# Purpose

This document defines every human and AI persona that interacts with AGE.

It establishes:

- Who they are
- Why they use AGE
- Their responsibilities
- Their workflows
- Their decision authority
- Their KPIs
- Their dashboards
- Their AI assistance
- Their permissions
- Their reports
- Their notifications
- Their automation requirements

This document is the foundation for:

- Permission Model
- Navigation
- Dashboard Design
- Automation Engine
- Reporting Engine
- AI Agent Architecture
- Capability Design

---

# Core Design Principles

Every persona exists for one purpose:

> Make better business decisions faster.

AGE should never force users to search for information.

Instead, AGE should proactively provide:

- Opportunities
- Risks
- Recommendations
- Evidence
- Trends
- Business Impact
- Suggested Next Actions

Every dashboard must answer three questions immediately:

1. What needs my attention?
2. Why does it matter?
3. What should I do next?

# Standard Persona Template

Every persona follows the exact structure below.

1. **Persona Overview** — Name, Department, Reports To, Primary Objective, Secondary Objectives, Business Value
2. **Responsibilities** — Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly
3. **Decision Authority** — decisions made independently; decisions requiring approval
4. **Daily Workflow** — Morning, Mid-Day, Afternoon, Evening
5. **Weekly Workflow** — Weekly planning, Reviews, Client interactions, Internal meetings, Learning, Optimization
6. **Monthly Workflow** — Performance review, Business review, Strategic planning, Budget review, Capability review
7. **Inputs Required** — information required before work begins
8. **Outputs Produced** — everything this persona creates
9. **Dashboards** — Purpose, Widgets, KPIs, Alerts, Drill-down capability
10. **Reports** — Reports received, Reports created, Report frequency
11. **Notifications** — Critical, Important, Informational, Digest, Escalation
12. **AI Agents** — Agent Name, Responsibility, Inputs, Outputs, Confidence Threshold, Escalation Rules, Human Approval Requirements
13. **Permissions** — Read, Create, Update, Delete, Approve, Export, Admin
14. **Integrations Used** — e.g. GSC, GA, Google Ads, Meta Ads, LinkedIn Ads, GitHub, SSH, CRM, Email, Slack, Teams
15. **KPIs** — Primary, Secondary, Leading Indicators, Lagging Indicators
16. **Pain Points** — current operational problems
17. **Success Criteria** — what success looks like
18. **Automation Opportunities** — repetitive work AGE should automate
19. **Collaboration Matrix** — Human collaborators, AI collaborators, Upstream dependencies, Downstream dependencies
20. **Audit Requirements** — Decision Maker, Timestamp, Evidence Used, AI Recommendation, Final Decision, Business Outcome

## Executive Leadership

### Founder / CEO

> **Persona Status:** 🟢 Complete — populated during Product Design.

#### 1. Persona Overview

**Role**

Founder / CEO

**Department**

Executive Leadership

**Reports To**

Board of Directors (if applicable)

**Primary Objective**

Build a profitable, scalable, AI-first growth company that consistently delivers measurable business outcomes for clients.

**Secondary Objectives**

- Grow recurring revenue
- Increase client retention
- Improve operational efficiency
- Build competitive differentiation
- Expand into new markets
- Continuously improve service quality

**Business Value**

Acts as the final strategic decision maker and is accountable for the overall success of the business.

#### 2. Responsibilities

##### Daily

- Review executive dashboard
- Review critical alerts
- Approve strategic decisions
- Review AI executive brief
- Monitor revenue and delivery health

##### Weekly

- Leadership meeting
- Client health review
- Sales pipeline review
- Delivery review
- Growth review

##### Monthly

- Financial review
- Strategic review
- Team performance review
- Market intelligence review
- Product roadmap review

##### Quarterly

- Company goals
- Budget planning
- Market expansion
- Service expansion
- Organizational planning

#### 3. Decision Authority

Can independently approve:

- Business strategy
- Budgets
- Hiring
- Investments
- Partnerships
- Service offerings
- Pricing
- Client escalations

Requires board approval only where legally applicable.

#### 4. Daily Workflow

##### Morning

- Executive Brief generated by AGE
- Business Health Score
- Revenue Snapshot
- Critical Risks
- Top Opportunities
- Client Health
- Market Intelligence Summary

##### Mid-Day

- Leadership reviews
- Decision approvals
- Escalation handling

##### Afternoon

- Strategy discussions
- Growth initiatives
- Client reviews

##### Evening

- Daily summary
- Pending approvals
- Tomorrow's priorities

#### 5. Weekly Workflow

Review:

- Revenue
- Pipeline
- Client Health
- Delivery Performance
- Operational Efficiency
- Marketing Performance
- AI Recommendations

Approve:

- Strategic initiatives
- Budget changes
- Hiring requests

#### 6. Monthly Workflow

Review:

- Profitability
- Client retention
- Department KPIs
- Team utilization
- Market positioning
- Competitor movement
- Product improvements

#### 7. Inputs Required

- Business Intelligence Framework
- Strategy Intelligence
- Research Intelligence
- Financial Metrics
- Client KPIs
- Sales Pipeline
- Operations Dashboard
- Market Intelligence
- Executive Reports

#### 8. Outputs Produced

- Strategic Decisions
- Budget Approvals
- Company Objectives
- Growth Priorities
- Executive Approvals
- Leadership Communication
- Quarterly Goals

#### 9. Dashboards

**Primary Dashboard:** Executive Command Center

Contains:

- Business Health Score
- Revenue
- Profit
- Pipeline
- Client Health
- Delivery Health
- Team Capacity
- Market Signals
- Strategic Opportunities
- High Priority Risks
- AI Recommendations

#### 10. Reports

Receives:

- Daily Executive Brief
- Weekly Leadership Report
- Monthly Business Review
- Quarterly Strategic Report
- Annual Growth Report

#### 11. Notifications

Only high-value notifications.

Examples:

- Revenue anomaly
- Critical client risk
- Campaign overspend
- Major ranking loss
- Security incident
- SLA breach
- Executive approval required

#### 12. AI Agents

Primary Agents:

- Executive Agent
- Strategy Agent
- Intelligence Agent
- Reporting Agent
- Decision Support Agent

Each recommendation must include:

- Evidence
- Confidence Score
- Business Impact
- Recommended Action
- Expected Outcome

#### 13. Permissions

Full platform access.

Cannot bypass:

- Audit logging
- Approval history
- Evidence tracking

#### 14. Integrations Used

- Google Analytics
- Google Search Console
- Google Ads
- Meta Ads
- LinkedIn Ads
- CRM
- GitHub
- SSH
- Accounting Platform
- Email
- Calendar
- Slack / Teams

#### 15. KPIs

- Revenue Growth
- MRR
- ARR
- Gross Margin
- EBITDA
- Client Retention
- Net Revenue Retention
- Average Project Margin
- Pipeline Value
- Conversion Rate
- Employee Utilization
- Customer Satisfaction
- NPS

#### 16. Pain Points

- Disconnected data
- Slow decision making
- Too many dashboards
- Reactive reporting
- Lack of evidence
- Difficulty prioritizing
- Poor visibility across clients

#### 17. Success Criteria

The CEO should understand the complete health of the business within five minutes of logging into AGE.

Every recommendation should explain:

- What happened
- Why it happened
- Business impact
- Recommended action
- Expected outcome

#### 18. Automation Opportunities

- Executive Brief
- Board Reports
- Risk Detection
- Opportunity Detection
- Budget Monitoring
- Revenue Forecasting
- Client Risk Monitoring
- Competitive Monitoring
- Weekly Leadership Packs
- Quarterly Reviews

#### 19. Collaboration Matrix

Works Closely With:

- COO
- Growth Director
- Sales
- Account Managers
- Project Managers
- Finance

AI Agents:

- Executive Agent
- Strategy Agent
- Reporting Agent
- Research Agent

#### 20. Audit Requirements

Every executive decision must record:

- Decision
- Decision Maker
- Timestamp
- Supporting Evidence
- AI Recommendation
- Confidence Score
- Final Outcome
- Business Impact
- Linked Strategy
- Related Objectives
- Approval History
